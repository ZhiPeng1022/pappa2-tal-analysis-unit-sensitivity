## Scientific correction

Donor-within reversal was 19.0% in the target panel versus 12.7% in
expression-matched sets. After preserving effect-size marginals and breaking
gene-wise unit pairing, the conditional null was 20.8% (`P = 0.999`).

The archive therefore does not claim broad donor-within negative coupling.
The analysis-unit disagreement itself remains observable and aTAL2 remains the
clearest case example.
