# Pre-submission inquiry to Genome Medicine

Date: 29 October 2026

Subject: Pre-submission inquiry: unit-aware analysis of single-cell kidney
disease states

Dear Editors,

We are considering submitting our manuscript, "Unit-aware analysis reveals
opposing donor and within-donor disease signals in human kidney single-cell
data", as a Research Article to Genome Medicine.

Single-cell disease studies often describe a cell state as disease-associated
without specifying whether the signal reflects donor composition, local
neighborhood structure, or coupling within a donor. We developed a unit-aware
framework and evaluated it in 106,851 human kidney thick ascending limb cells
from 223 donors. In aTAL2, donor-level and neighborhood-expression directions
were positive while the within-donor association was negative. A prespecified
400-gene panel showed donor-within reversal in 19.0% of genes versus 12.7% of
expression-matched random sets. A conditional effect-pairing null gave 20.8%,
showing that the initial excess reflected the selected effect-size
distribution rather than broad negative coupling between units. A smaller
neighborhood-versus-within excess remained threshold sensitive. A simulation
benchmark distinguished true opposing effects from concordant and null
scenarios.

Two non-KPMP cohorts supported donor-level PAPPA2 disease elevation, although
exact subtype replication and the external within-donor effect were not
obtained. Kidney tubulointerstitial eQTL, PTWAS, and snATAC analyses are
reported as regulatory context and boundary evidence rather than causal proof.
The analysis code and derived data will be archived with a new persistent DOI
before formal submission.

We believe this work fits Genome Medicine because it addresses a general
measurement problem in genomic medicine and provides a reusable framework for
cell-state studies across diseases.

The authors have no publication funding. Before submitting, may we ask whether
the manuscript would be considered within scope and whether a discretionary
APC waiver could be considered if the manuscript is sent for review? Genome
