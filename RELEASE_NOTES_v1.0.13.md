## Scientific boundary

The aTAL2/PAPPA2 cell-rank conflict is stable across UMAP k, seed, and
proportion settings but is embedding-sensitive. The donor-conditioned
panel-level residual changes direction in panel PCA30. The study therefore
defines calibration stability as a reporting standard rather than claiming a
universal calibrated-null result.
